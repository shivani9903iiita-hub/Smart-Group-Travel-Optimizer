## Travel Planning Systems

### Wanderlog — Independent Review (AI Feature Test)

**Source Type:** Independent review

**Title:** Wanderlog Review 2026 — AI Trip Planner Tested on a Real Istanbul Itinerary

**URL:** https://aitravel.tools/wanderlog-review/

**Accessed:** 21 August 2026

**Why this source was used:**

To understand how Wanderlog's AI assistant actually generates an
itinerary in practice — what input it takes and what output it
produces — rather than relying only on marketing claims from the
official site.

**Key information collected:**

- The AI takes a single free-text prompt describing the whole trip
  and group at once (not separate inputs per traveler).
- Generates one day-by-day itinerary per prompt; refinements happen
  through follow-up chat messages (limited to 5 free messages/trip).
- Budget is estimated *after* the itinerary is generated, as a
  breakdown — not used as a constraint during itinerary generation.
- No feature to generate multiple alternative itineraries
  (e.g. budget-first vs. preference-first) from the same inputs.
- AI-suggested data can be outdated (reviewer found a 2023 data
  cutoff causing factual errors on prices and site status).

### TripIt — Independent Review

**Source Type:** Independent review

**Title:** TripIt Review 2026: The Complete Honest Assessment

**URL:** https://www.going.com/guides/tripit-review

**Accessed:** 21 August 2026

**Why this source was used:**

To understand TripIt's core functionality and how it handles
multi-traveler trips, since TripIt is widely known as a
booking-organization tool rather than a planning tool.

**Key information collected:**

- Builds a chronological itinerary from forwarded booking
  confirmation emails or automatic inbox scanning (Inbox Sync).
- No AI or recommendation feature — it organizes only what the
  user has already booked, nothing is suggested or generated.
- Group/sharing feature lets others view or collaborate on a
  shared itinerary; multiple people's synced bookings merge into
  one itinerary automatically.
- No budget tracking, preference matching, or optimization logic
  of any kind.
